What is coaching, and is it right for me?

Coaching gives you clarity on what matters, and a clear next step you own.

It isn’t about being given answers.
Mentoring hands you advice, and consulting hands you a solution. Coaching helps you work out your own.

Questions people ask

Who is this for?

Tech leaders and managers who want to step up, and career changers and early-career professionals who want a clear direction. If you're capable but unsure of the next move, this is for you.

What if I’m not sure coaching is right for me?

Start with the two-minute Clarity Check, or book the free intro call. There is no obligation either way.

How does coaching work?

It starts with a free intro call, a relaxed conversation to get to know each other and see if we click. If we both want to go ahead, we sign a short agreement covering confidentiality and the other terms. Then we meet for sessions, ideally once a month. Life happens, so extra casual sessions are available during that period, subject to my availability.

How is coaching different from mentoring?

A mentor shares what worked for them. A coach helps you find your own answers and act on them. I've done both, and coaching is how I help you unlock what's already strong in you. See what coaching is.

What happens on the free intro call?

It's a relaxed 30-minute conversation to get to know each other and see if we click. I'll learn a little about where you are and where you want to go. No pitch.

How do I reschedule or cancel a call?

Use the link in your booking confirmation email. If you can’t, email me at leadwithfx@gmail.com, ideally at least 72 hours before the call.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Come as you are. If it helps, think about one decision or goal that's on your mind.

How many sessions do I need?

I recommend four sessions over four months. That is where I find coaching goes beneath the surface and people start to unlock what they’re capable of.

How much does coaching cost?

It depends on what you need, so I talk it through with you on the free intro call.

Is coaching online or in person?

Online. Coaching is by video, from anywhere, within Australian working hours. In-person sessions can be arranged in Sydney CBD, at extra cost to cover the venue and travel.

Will you see my Clarity Check answers?

Only if you ask me to. Your answers stay in your browser unless you choose to have your full Clarity Report prepared and tick the box that lets me see them before a call. See the privacy page.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between us, within the limits of the law. If your organisation is funding the coaching, what is shared with them is agreed upfront and covers only what you are comfortable sharing. I'm completing my certification through IECL, and as a participant I'm held to the IECL Code of Conduct.

When might coaching not be the right fit?

Coaching isn’t therapy, so it isn’t the right place for mental health concerns. A GP or psychologist is the right first step there. It also isn’t for people who want me to hand over the answers (that’s mentoring or consulting), or who aren’t ready to make a change or put in the thinking between sessions. If coaching isn’t right for you at the moment, that’s fine. The intro call will help us both see that. And if we turn out not to be the right fit, the next question will help you find someone who is.

How do I choose the right coach?

Whoever you choose, ask them: What training do you have, and what is still in progress? What code of conduct do you work to? How do you keep what I share confidential? What does a session look like? Is there a free first call with no hard sell? Be wary of anyone who guarantees results or promises to hand you the answers. I’m happy to answer every one of these, including that my IECL certification is in progress. If we’re not the right fit, I’d rather you find someone who is.

How can I contact you?

Email me at leadwithfx@gmail.com. I’ll reply as soon as I see it, but please allow up to 72 hours. If you’d rather talk, book a free intro call.
