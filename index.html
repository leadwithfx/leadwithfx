When might coaching not be the right fit?

Coaching isn’t therapy, so it isn’t the right place for mental health concerns. A GP or psychologist is the right first step there. It also isn’t for people who want me to hand over the answers (that’s mentoring or consulting), or who aren’t ready to make a change or put in the thinking between sessions. If we talk and I don’t think I’m the right fit, I’ll tell you honestly.

How does coaching work?

It starts with a free intro call, a relaxed conversation to get to know each other and see if we click. If we both want to go ahead, we sign a short agreement covering confidentiality and the other terms. Then we meet for sessions, ideally once a month. Life happens, so extra casual sessions are available during that period, subject to my availability.

How many sessions do I need?

I recommend four sessions over four months. That is where I find coaching goes beneath the surface and people start to unlock what they’re capable of.
