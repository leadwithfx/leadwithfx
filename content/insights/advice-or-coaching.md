When you are stuck, the first question is not who to ask. It is what kind of help you need. The three most common kinds get mixed up, and that is why so much help does not help.

## Three kinds of help

- **A mentor shares what worked for them.** Useful when someone has walked your exact path.
- **A consultant hands you a solution.** Useful when you have a defined problem and no time.
- **A coach helps you find your own answer.** Useful when the problem is unclear, or you know what to do and are not doing it.

## How to choose

1. If you can describe the problem in one sentence, you probably need a consultant or a mentor.
2. If you keep circling the same question without moving, you probably need a coach.
3. If you are not sure which, say that out loud to someone you trust and notice which kind of question helps.
