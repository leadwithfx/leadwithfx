Most people in tech do not get stuck because they stop working hard. They get stuck because the thing that worked for the last ten years quietly stops working.

## The pattern

You were promoted for being the person who gets things done. So you take on more, say yes faster and fix problems yourself. At some point, the role changes and the same behaviour starts to cap you.

[YOUR STORY: a moment from your own career when this happened to you or someone you led. Keep it to three or four sentences and leave out names and company details.]

## Three signs it is happening

- You are busier than ever, but the work you are noticed for has not changed.
- Decisions that matter are made in rooms you are not in.
- You can describe your tasks clearly, but not the outcome you want in two years.

## What to try this week

1. Write down the one result you want in twelve months, in one sentence.
2. List what you do today that someone else could do, and pick one thing to hand over.
3. Ask one person whose opinion matters what they think you are known for.
