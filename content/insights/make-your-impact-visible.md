Many strong people assume that if they do excellent work, it will be noticed. Sometimes it is. Often it is not, because the people deciding about promotions and projects are busy and see only a small part of what you do.

## Visibility is not self-promotion

It is making sure the right people can answer one question: what has this person changed?

[YOUR STORY: a time good work went unseen, or a time a small change in how you shared results made a difference. Three or four sentences, no names.]

## A simple routine

- **Say the outcome, not the task.** "Cut the onboarding time for new teams" lands better than "worked on onboarding".
- **Share progress early and briefly.** A short note to the people who care, once a month, is enough.
- **Name who benefited.** It shows the impact is real and makes it easy for others to repeat it.

## What to try this week

1. Write three outcomes you delivered this quarter, each in one line with a number if you honestly have one.
2. Choose the one person who most influences your next step and share one of them.
3. Ask them what they would like to hear about next.
