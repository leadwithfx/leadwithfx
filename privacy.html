Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Lead with FX (leadwithfx.com) is operated by Freddie Xavier in Sydney, Australia. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used. I aim to collect as little as possible.

What this website collects

  • Anonymous visit statistics. I use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not build profiles of individuals. It records things like the page visited, the referring site, browser type and country.
  • Bookings. If you book an intro call, you enter your details (such as name and email) on Calendly. Calendly processes those details under its own privacy policy, and I receive them so I can run the call.
  • Emails you send me. If you email leadwithfx@gmail.com, I keep your message and address so I can reply.
  • The Clarity Check. Your answers are used in your browser to show your result. They are not sent to me or stored on a server.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, analytics from GoatCounter, and the booking calendar from Calendly. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads their content. Each has its own privacy policy.

How I use your information

To respond to you, run coaching conversations, and understand how the site is used so I can improve it. I do not sell your information. I will only send you marketing emails if you have asked for them, and every such email will include a way to unsubscribe.

Clarity Check and Clarity Report

The Clarity Check runs in your browser. Your answers stay there unless you choose to share them with me.

The on-screen result shows only part of your Clarity Report. If you share your answers, I receive them with your first name and email address, and I use them to prepare your full report and go through it with you on an intro call. I email you one confirmation. I do not email you the report. The answer choices (never your name or email) are sent to an AI service, Anthropic, to write a short reflection, and the email is sent through an email service provider. These services process the information on my behalf and may do so outside Australia. I keep your answers only in the email sent to me, to prepare for our call.

If you also tick the box saying I can contact you personally, I keep your first name, email address, your result, your profile choice and whether you have worked with a coach, so I can follow up with you one to one. I do not send a newsletter. Any follow-up message will tell you how to stop hearing from me. You can ask me to delete your details at any time by emailing leadwithfx@gmail.com. If you do not tick that box, I do not keep your details.

When you click “Book a free intro call”, your result, your profile choice and whether you have worked with a coach are added to the booking link, so I can see them if you book. They are sent to Calendly only if you click through to book.

Confidentiality of coaching

What you share in coaching is kept confidential, within the limits of the law.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you by emailing leadwithfx@gmail.com. If you have a concern about how your information is handled, contact me first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
